# Philosopher music files

The player looks for these authorized recordings at runtime:

- `plato.mp3` — contemplative ancient Greek lyre reconstruction
- `aristotle.mp3` — instrumental *Seikilos Epitaph* reconstruction
- `machiavelli.mp3` — an authorized recording supplied for the private demonstration
- `hume.mp3` — public-domain or appropriately licensed harpsichord recording of Handel's *Sarabande in D minor, HWV 437*
- `kant.mp3` — public-domain or appropriately licensed recording of the second movement of Mozart's Piano Sonata No. 16 in C major, K. 545

The five supplied recordings are now present in this directory. Confirm that
the recordings are authorized for the course demonstration before publishing
the project publicly. Missing files are handled gracefully by the frontend;
the game remains fully playable without music. Do not add an unlicensed
recording of *Ezio's Family* or any other copyrighted performance.
